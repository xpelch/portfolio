# Vision

## Destination

Build a bilingual developer portfolio that makes Xavier Pelchat feel like a
production-minded full-stack engineer: clear work, credible proof, practical AI
workflow signal, and a fast path to contact.

## User / Operator

- Who this must help: engineering managers, founders, technical recruiters, and
  collaborators evaluating Xavier for full-stack product work.
- Job to be done: understand Xavier's stack, judgment, work style, selected
  projects, and contact path in under two minutes.
- Success moment: the visitor sees credible proof of production delivery and
  either opens a project or starts contact.

## Success Metrics

- Activation metric: first viewport exposes name, role, offer, proof strip, and
  contact/work CTAs without requiring scroll.
- Quality metric: desktop and mobile screenshots show no overflow, clipped
  text, broken accents, broken anchors, or missing images.
- Reliability/proof metric: `npm test` passes locally and in CI, covering
  translation contracts, selected work evidence, links, lint, and production
  build.

## Product Principles

- Make the work the primary signal.
- Treat agentic/AI as operating evidence, not the main character.
- Prefer human, specific, proof-backed copy over generic portfolio slogans.
- Preserve bilingual content parity.
- Keep the page fast, accessible, and easy to verify.

## Non-Goals

- A chatbot-first portfolio.
- Fake AI autonomy claims.
- Decorative complexity that hides the projects.
- Confidential employer or client details.

## Definition of Done

- A complete visitor journey is proven: land, understand, inspect work, switch
  language, and contact.
- The canonical proof command passes: `npm test`.
- Desktop/mobile visual artifacts exist under `logs/visual/`.
- Autogrowth score is at least 90/100 or the remaining blocker is documented
  with a concrete next proof lane.
